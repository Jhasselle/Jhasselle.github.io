Side-bet probabilities

How often the NPC players take each side bet. 0% = never, 100% = every round.

Hand quality

How often a hand is dealt “hot” — stacked toward something that pays, instead of taken off the top. Premium hands stay rare even at 100%: a royal shows up on roughly one hot hand in 200, five wilds about one in 2,000.

JACKPOT

Meter hit — nothing comes out of the rack. Lock up the hand, call the floor, and move on.

Payouts

Every paytable for Deuces Wild Xtreme. The Joker and all four deuces are fully wild.

Hand ranking

Not standard poker order — Five Wilds is the top hand, and a Royal outranks Five of a Kind. Ties are not broken by wild vs natural.

Ante & Play

Ante and Odds are equal and mandatory. The player either folds — losing both — or makes the Play bet at twice the Ante. Beat the dealer and Ante and Play pay even money. Tie and all three push. There is no dealer qualifier.
